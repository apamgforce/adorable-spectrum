# Volunteer Coordinator Handbook

You run Greenforce's volunteers from one page: **greenforceafrica.com/admin**. It works on a phone or a laptop. Sign in with the username and password you were given, and never share them. If someone else needs to help with photos or website numbers, give them their own login (see *Helper logins* below).

The dashboard has a short guide built in. Press **How to use this** at the top.

---

## How a volunteer joins

1. They apply at **greenforceafrica.com/volunteer** (name, WhatsApp, email, track, hours, mode). It takes a minute.
2. They appear at the top of your **Volunteers** tab, marked *New applicant* in orange. The number on the first card at the top tells you how many are waiting.
3. You tap their name to look at their details, then press **Approve**.
4. A green box appears. Press **Send on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with a ready message containing their volunteer ID, the Active Volunteers group link, the training link and the portal link. Press send.
5. They join the group, do the training and open the portal with their ID.

Not a good fit? Press **Decline**. They move to *Paused* and can be approved later.

> The welcome message only includes the group link if it's saved under **Settings → Active Volunteers group link**. Set this once.

## Your weekly routine (30 minutes to 2 hours)

**1. Approve new applicants.** As above.

**2. Give tasks.** Open **Give a task**.
- Choose a ready-made task from the first dropdown, or write your own title and instructions.
- Pick a track and a due date.
- On the right, tick the volunteers. *All* ticks everyone. The track button ticks only people in that track.
- Press **Give to … selected**.
- Post in the Active Volunteers group: "New tasks are live. Open the portal with your ID."

**3. Chase.** Open **Tasks** and tap a task. You see who has finished and who hasn't. Press **Remind** next to a name to send a polite WhatsApp nudge. Use **Remove** to take a task off someone, or *Add another volunteer* to give it to one more person.

**4. Check the work.** Open **Verify**. Each item shows what the volunteer sent (text, photos, files) and whether they typed DONE in the group.
- Good: press **Verify**.
- Needs fixing: press **Send back**, then **Chat** and tell them what to change. The task goes back to their list.

**5. Certificates (once a month).** Open **Certificates**. A green *Qualifies* tag means they have served 2+ months, had 4+ tasks and finished at least half. Press **Create certificate**, then **Email it** or **WhatsApp it**.

## Keeping records correct

Tap any volunteer's name to open their details. You can change their name, WhatsApp number, email, track, hours per month, engagement mode and status, and keep **Private notes** only admins can see. Press **Save changes**.

- WhatsApp numbers: Ghana numbers can be typed as `024 123 4567`. Other countries need `+` and the country code, e.g. `+220 712 3456`.
- **Paused** means they can't open the portal and won't be offered tasks. Use it when someone steps back. You can make them Active again any time.
- **Delete** removes the person and all their tasks, work and certificate forever. Use it only for duplicates and test entries.
- **Add** (Volunteers tab) creates someone who didn't apply on the website. They're approved straight away and get an ID.
- **Search** finds people by name, email, phone or ID. The two dropdowns filter by status and track.
- **Export** downloads the list you're looking at as a spreadsheet. Download the full list once a month as a backup.

Changes you make here also update the contact in HubSpot when HubSpot is connected. If a volunteer shows *CRM not updated*, open them and save again, or press **Send missing details to HubSpot** in Settings.

## Settings

- **Active Volunteers group link.** In WhatsApp: open the group → tap the group name → *Invite via link* → *Copy link*. Paste it here and press Save. If you ever reset the group link in WhatsApp, paste the new one here too.
- **Helper logins.** Create a login for a trusted person. *Gallery manager* can only upload and manage website photos at `/gallery-admin`. *Insights viewer* can only see website visits at `/insights`. Neither can see volunteers. Remove a login when the person no longer helps. Typing an existing username with a new password resets their password.

## What volunteers see

- **/volunteer/portal**: they type their ID and see their tasks. They press *Mark as done*, attach photos, files or text, then type DONE in the Active Volunteers group and press *I typed DONE*.
- Their **Verified Volunteer badge**, which they can add to LinkedIn, download or share.
- Their **certificate**, once you create it.
- **/volunteer/training**: five short modules and the weekly cycle. Send it to anyone who is unsure how things work.

## If something goes wrong

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| A volunteer lost their ID | Volunteers tab → search them → **Send ID**. It's the same ID. |
| "We couldn't find that ID" | Check they're *Active*. New applicants and paused volunteers can't log in. Check for typos: IDs look like `GF-7K2QX`. |
| WhatsApp button is missing | They have no valid number. Open them and fix it. |
| WhatsApp opens the wrong person | The number is wrong or missing its country code. Fix it in their details. |
| Someone applied twice | Same email: their details were simply updated. Different emails: keep one, delete the other. |
| A task went to the wrong person | Tasks → open the task → **Remove** beside their name. |
| A task was a mistake | Tasks → open it → **Delete task**. |
| Numbers look out of date | Press the refresh arrow at the top. |
| You can't sign in | Check capitals and spaces. If the password was changed, ask whoever manages the website. |

## Good habits

- Give clear titles, step-by-step instructions and a due date. Keep each task under 2 hours.
- Match tasks to tracks so people do work they signed up for.
- Read anything AI writes before you assign it.
- Verify within a few days. Quick feedback keeps volunteers active.
- Protect beneficiaries: don't approve photo tasks that show children's faces without consent.
